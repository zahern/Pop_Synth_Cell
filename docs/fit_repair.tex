\paragraph{Repair-stage fit.}
The six configurations above vary the pool and the fitting method.  A second
full-cohort comparison isolates the candidate repair.  Both arms use the
corrected 19-table controls, the same 362 zones, the same integer program and
the same solver settings; only the candidate pool differs.
\Cref{tab:fit_repair} reports the result.  With the CP pool the repair gives
median 0.0127 and mean 0.0201; the donor-balanced pool lowers these to 0.0106
and 0.0151 and is better in 286 of 362 paired zones.  The 5\% trimmed mean
falls from 0.0151 to 0.0126 and the 95th percentile from 0.0433 to 0.0375, so
the gain is not confined to the tail; only three zones are worse by more than
0.01.  Every control table has a lower mean RSSZ except hours worked, dwelling
structure and mortgage repayment, where the absolute change is below 0.0001.
The largest reductions are in personal income, travel to work, field of study,
qualification level, household income and education institution.
Pooled TVD falls from 0.0184 to 0.0170 and held-out RSSZ from 0.0436 to 0.0420.
Joint TVD is essentially unchanged, rising from 0.2317 to 0.2346 (1.3\%): the
donor households are selected for their marginal contribution, not their joint
profile.  The remaining residual is concentrated in student status and
relationship in household.  For relationship, part of the target is overseas
visitors outside the private-dwelling pool; in the six-zone pilot the fitted
RLHP TVD was 6.25\% on all categories but 2.50\% on the resident universe.
