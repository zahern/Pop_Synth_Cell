Travel-survey validation

The fitted population is checked against the attached Queensland Travel Survey attributes: household bicycles, e-bikes and e-scooters, and person car and motorcycle licences. The panels show fitted minus attached-pool differences as heatmaps by household composition, household size, age band and reference/member age, spatial differences by SA4 and zone-size quintile, observed-donor coverage, and the spatial age distribution across SA2s.

What each method is

The method has three stages. CP construction builds a rule-feasible candidate pool; the expansion MIP adds donor--shell households and constructed carriers for zero-capacity cells; the fitting MIP then selects households from the resulting pool. The six configurations compare the CP pool with SA and IPU, and the expanded pool with SA, IPU, MIP and unique MIP.
