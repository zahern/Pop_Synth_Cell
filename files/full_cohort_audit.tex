\subsection{Joint and diversity audit}
\label{sec:full_audit}
Table~\ref{tab:full_audit} reports the joint and diversity audit for the eight
completed expansion arms and the one-MIP arm on the same 367 fitted zones.
Joint TVD is the mean per-zone total-variation distance over the eight designed
attribute pairs; $|\Delta V|$ is the mean absolute difference in Cram\'{e}r's
$V$ on those pairs; identical persons are persons whose 20-attribute tuple
repeats inside the same zone; cross-zone cloning is the share of distinct
tuples that appear in more than one zone. Lower values are better except for
the marginal fit, where the RSSZ columns repeat the 11-table result.

No arm dominates. Table~\ref{tab:full_audit} shows the trade-off: SA-Only
retains the lowest joint TVD and association error, the current MIP IPU arm has
the lowest within-zone duplication, and the one-MIP arm has the lowest marginal
median but the highest within-zone duplication and joint TVD. Integer
replication reproduces whole households many times, so its marginal advantage is
bought by duplication rather than by a richer joint structure; its lower
cross-zone cloning reflects the smaller distinct-tuple set, not greater
diversity. The composition-aware variants improve marginal RSSZ but raise
duplication, so the marginal gain is not free: it is bought by reusing a smaller
set of tuples. The trade-off is reported rather than folded into a single
ranking.
