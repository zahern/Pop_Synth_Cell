\subsection{Composition control in expansion}
\label{sec:expansion_composition}
The mixed-integer program in Section~\ref{sec:mip_expansion_formulation}
optimises coverage and marginal composition jointly, but only over the support
set it selects. To expose the distinction, a 300-donor, 32-shell build was
produced with the full menu appended (\texttt{--append-menu-candidates}); it is
the full-menu stress case in Table~\ref{tab:expansion_composition}. In that
build, the selected set contributes
2.9\% of the added persons; the other 97.1\% come from appending the remaining
candidate menu after the solve. The appended
records are not part of the admission vector $y$, are not in the deviation terms
$d_{k,m}$, and are not subject to the composition tie-break. The result is a
pool that is cell-complete but compositionally skewed: the 300-donor build has
8.7\% size-7 households and 6.9\% \texttt{mtwp} code 17 (``Not stated''),
against 5.0\% and 0.0\% in the current pool.

Two composition-controlled variants were built from the same shards, without
rerunning the MIP. The \emph{coverage-only} variant keeps the selected support
set and drops the appended menu. The \emph{composition-aware} variant keeps the
base pool, the selected set and only those appended households that are neither
size 7 nor contain \texttt{mtwp} code 17. The implementation also exposes a
composition-weight multiplier, optional size-7/\texttt{mtwp} caps and a
minimum-admitted-household lower bound; hard caps alone can starve coverage (a
test with a 1\% \texttt{mtwp} cap selected only one household), so the reported
variants use the two filters above. Table~\ref{tab:expansion_composition}
reports their composition and fitted RSSZ.

{\footnotesize\textit{Note:} the 2{,}000-donor rows report composition only; no fit was run on that pool. The filter removes every size-7 and \texttt{mtwp}-17 household by construction, so its 0.0\% entries are definitions rather than outcomes.}

A 2{,}000-donor, 32-shell build was completed as a separate stress test. With
the full menu appended it produces 3{,}576{,}519 persons and 1{,}207{,}317
households, but 32.0\% of persons and 85.8\% of households carry \texttt{mtwp}
code 17 and 21.8\% of households are size 7. The composition filter keeps
254{,}327 persons (11.7\% of the appended total) and 92{,}888 households,
giving 1{,}650{,}669 persons and 642{,}587 households: 2.5\% more persons than
the 300-donor composition-aware pool. The additional donor sample is therefore
consumed by verbatim census households that the filter removes. The build is
reported as a negative result: it does not deliver a substantially larger clean
pool, and it is not used to claim a larger-pool effect.

The composition-controlled variants therefore separate the two questions the
300-donor full-menu build conflated: whether expansion covers the zero-capacity cells, and
whether the added records preserve the census composition. The coverage-only
variant answers the first while removing the second's skew; the
composition-aware variant asks whether a larger pool can be added without
importing the not-stated and size-7 surplus.

The fitted result identifies the mechanism. On the 367-zone cohort, the
composition-aware pool scores an SA mean and median of 0.250 and 0.158, and an
IPU mean and median of 0.302 and 0.196. The current MIP-expanded pool scores
0.511 and 0.295 under SA and 0.315 and 0.193 under IPU; the 300-donor full-menu
build scores 1.822 and 1.168 under SA and 1.127 and 0.789 under IPU; SA-Only
scores 0.247 and 0.233, and IPU-Only 0.435 and 0.275. The composition-controlled
pool therefore removes the SA penalty: its SA mean is within 0.003 of SA-Only
while its median is 0.075 lower. Under IPU it lowers the mean from 0.315 to
0.302, although the median is essentially unchanged (0.193 to 0.196). The
coverage-only pool scores 0.329 and 0.177 under SA, and 0.335 and 0.214 under
IPU; it also recovers most of the penalty, but the composition-aware append
keeps substantially more candidates at the same composition. The difference between the two controlled
variants is therefore pool size, not composition: both remove the
appended-menu skew.
